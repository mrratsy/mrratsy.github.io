Mr Ratsy

A watercolor picture-book about a brave little mouse.

A little mouse with a bundle on a stick steps through the tooth-arch — and the wide, watercolour world begins.

*Adventures of Mr Ratsy* is a picture-book for small humans (and the grown-ups reading aloud). Painted in soft watercolour, it follows Mr Ratsy on a journey full of small bravery, quiet wonder, and the kind of friends you only meet when you finally leave home.
